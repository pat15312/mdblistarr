Whole-title cleanup removes associated media files through Arr, then removes the movie or series catalogue entry. It shares each product's existing deletion-attempt cap with duplicate cleanup. Empty folders and unrelated sidecar files are left in place. Unsafe activity or Arr deletion settings defer removal; see Arr Health for the reason.

Use Arr Health to inspect pending titles, blocked removals and deletion history. Removing a title from all configured lists cancels pending removal and allows normal reconciliation to resume. Previously deleted titles are not restored automatically.
